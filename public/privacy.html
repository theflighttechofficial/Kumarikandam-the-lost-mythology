Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

This atlas has no accounts, no sign-up and no advertising. We do not sell or share personal data.

Stored on your device

Your research notes, tasks and the sources you mark as read are saved in your browser's local storage so they are still there next time. They never leave your device. Clear your browser's site data to delete them.

Visit statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites and does not store IP addresses. It records the page visited, the referring site, and your browser, operating system and country.

Fonts

Typefaces load from Google Fonts. Your browser sends its IP address to Google to fetch them, as with any website that uses Google Fonts. See Google's privacy policy.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies, so it shows no cookie banner.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs for security and reliability.
